## 🌟 About the Artist

> **Jon Kabir** (born Jon Christopher Kabir) is an iconic Bangladeshi rock singer, songwriter, and musician, widely celebrated as the legendary former frontman and co-founder of the seminal alternative rock band **Black**, and later the founder and frontman of alternative/indie outfit **Indalo**. Known for his distinctive soulful baritone, poetic and introspective songwriting, and electrifying stage presence, Jon Kabir shaped the sound of youth culture in Bangladesh during the 2000s and 2010s. His solo acoustic sessions—captured in *Rear-View*—strip back the distorted guitars to present raw, intimate reinterpretations of his most beloved classics alongside introspective solo works.

### 📖 About the Album
*Rear-View (Acoustic Sessions)* presents Jon Kabir in an intimate, unfiltered setting, performing stripped-down acoustic reimaginations of classic anthems originally recorded with Black. Featuring haunting, unadorned vocal delivery accompanied by pure acoustic guitar, the sessions offer a reflective retrospective on generational masterpieces like *"35"*, *"Onukkhon"*, *"Utshober Por"*, *"6th September"*, *"Shokarto Upokul"*, and *"Awdrishsho"*. These arrangements highlight the timeless songwriting, lyrical vulnerability, and enduring emotional depth of Bangladesh's 2000s alternative rock movement.

### 📖 About the Album
*Jewel With The Stars* is a renowned collaborative mixed album produced by guitar virtuoso Jewel, bringing together leading voices from Bangladesh's alternative and rock scenes. Jon Kabir contributed two standout performances: the introspective, melodic rock gem *"Jibon"* and the evocative power ballad *"Ek"*, demonstrating his versatile range outside traditional band arrangements.
